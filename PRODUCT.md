# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective academic collaborators, supervisors, admissions reviewers, research employers, and scholars who want a concise account of H. Zhan's research program, working papers, education, and applied research experience.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible bilingual academic identity in one continuous page. Success means visitors can understand the research agenda quickly, verify current work and credentials, and find a direct contact route without navigating repeated or fragmented pages.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, restrained, approachable. The page should feel precise and current while remaining natural in both Chinese and English.

## Anti-references

Avoid multi-page portfolio structures, oversized headings, repeated section summaries, prominent treatment of secondary work, excessive dividers, card-heavy layouts, inconsistent type sizes, mixed font roles, and decorative effects that compete with academic content.

## Design Principles

1. Put the research program and evidence ahead of decoration.
2. Remove repeated information and let every section add something new.
3. Keep the bilingual experience equivalent rather than mechanically identical.
4. Use a compact resume-like hierarchy with stable alignment and restrained emphasis.
5. Make contact details and scholarly identifiers easy to find without turning them into calls to action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and lists, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and native Chinese and Latin font stacks that remain readable across common devices.
